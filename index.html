Welcome aboard

A whole island of play, under one roof

Treasure Island is a kids' playground where children play, learn and explore through activities designed to build confidence, coordination and curiosity. From the ball-pit galleon to the craft table, every corner is made to be discovered.

  • Safe & supervised — padded surfaces, sanitised daily, trained play hosts on the floor at all times.
  • Learning through play — craft, role-play and sensory stations woven through the whole island.
  • Made for parents too — comfortable seating, clear sightlines and Le Gourmet dining right outside.

Your first visit

Simple as one, two, three

No membership, no complicated booking. Turn up, sock up, set them loose.

1

Find us on Level 2

Head to Galeries Lafayette inside The Dubai Mall and follow the signs to Le Gourmet — we're the blue clubhouse wrapped in greenery.

2

Check in & grip socks on

Register your little explorer at the desk. Anti-slip socks are required for play and available at reception if you forget yours.

3

Play, create, repeat

Our hosts are on the floor the whole time. Parents can settle in nearby with a coffee and a clear view of the island.

Weekends and school holidays get busy — message us ahead if you're bringing a group of five or more.
