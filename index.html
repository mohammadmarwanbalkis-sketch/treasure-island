Welcome aboard

A whole island of play, under one roof

Treasure Island is a kids' playground where children play, learn and explore through activities designed to build confidence, coordination and curiosity. From the ball-pit galleon to the craft table, every corner is made to be discovered.

  • Safe & supervised — padded surfaces, sanitised daily, trained play hosts on the floor at all times.
  • Learning through play — craft, role-play and sensory stations woven through the whole island.
  • Made for parents too — comfortable seating, clear sightlines and Le Gourmet dining right outside.
